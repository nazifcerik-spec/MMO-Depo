This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use MeNa World, under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What we process

  • Account: e-mail address, username, a one-way hash of your password (the password itself is never stored), when the account was created and last used, and the version of the terms you accepted.
  • Game data: characters, inventory, progress, quests, in-game economy records, marketplace activity.
  • Communication: chat messages, in-game mail, friend and block lists.
  • Technical: a one-way hash of your IP address, browser/app information (user agent), session records; if notifications are on, the device notification key, platform and language.

2. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating your account and providing the Game — performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security, fighting cheating and abuse, debugging — legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Account recovery and service e-mails — performance of a contract.
  • Legal obligations — KVKK Art. 5/2-ç; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not sell your data or profile you for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the Game: hosting, e-mail delivery and — if you turned notifications on — Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (APNs). These providers may be abroad; transfers use safeguards required by KVKK Art. 9 and the GDPR. We may share data with authorities where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account: after a 30-day grace period your e-mail, username and password hash are anonymised, and characters, inventory, notifications and device records are deleted. Transaction records are kept, no longer linkable to you, to protect the integrity of the in-game economy.
  • Chat messages: 30 days.
  • Backups: replaced by newer ones within 35 days.

5. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 and the GDPR you can access your data, ask for it to be corrected or erased, object to processing and take your data with you:

  • Access / portability: in the app, Account menu → “Download my data”. Everything we hold downloads as a JSON file.
  • Erasure: see “Account deletion” below.
  • Other requests: write to {{support_email}}; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

6. Account deletion

  1. Sign in to the app, choose “Delete my account” in the Account menu and confirm with your password.
  2. You are signed out on every device. Signing in again within 30 days cancels the deletion.
  3. After 30 days your data is deleted or anonymised as described under “How long we keep it”.

If you cannot reach the app, write from your registered e-mail address to {{support_email}} with the subject “Account deletion request”.

7. Children

The Game is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you notice such an account, please tell us.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are stored with a strong hashing algorithm (Argon2id), IP addresses are kept only as hashes, and access is limited to authorised staff.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will announce significant changes in the Game. Questions: {{operator}} · {{support_email}}
